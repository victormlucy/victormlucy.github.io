About me

Policy research grounded in evidence.

I’m Victor Lucy, a dual public policy and public administration student at the London School of Economics and Sciences Po. I work across policy analysis, economic research and data visualisation.

What I work on

My work brings together practical policy questions and evidence. Recent projects explore housing and clean electricity, alongside data visualisation work developed through LSE’s Automated Data Visualisation for Public Policy course.

Experience

I have worked on policy research and communications in government, the non-profit sector and political campaigns. My experience includes work with the OECD in Paris and organizations in British Columbia, Canada.
